01 / The commission

Made for one
individual.

A one-of-one custom automatic field watch, created for a specific recipient.

The project is intended to reflect discipline, precision, resilience and purpose. Its direction is informed by the recipient’s distinguished military and law-enforcement background, expressed through a practical format and considered details.

A custom design, hand-assembled using sourced components. The recipient’s story and the personal context of the commission will be recorded here when those details are provided.

02 / The inspiration

Three points of reference.

I / The field watch

Clarity of purpose.

Traditional mechanical field watches provide the foundation: direct time reading, Arabic numerals and a restrained hierarchy of information.

II / The recipient

A personal context.

The recipient’s military and law-enforcement background informs the character of the watch, with an emphasis on discipline, resilience and utility.

III / The visual language

Considered symbolism.

The design draws inspiration from the visual identity and philosophy of Bitfinex, expressed through selected motifs, color and dial details.

An independent personal project. No official affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement by Bitfinex is implied.

03 / Design philosophy

Legibility first.
Meaning in the details.

A matte black field-watch dial establishes a quiet foundation. The design intent balances clear time reading with small, specific references to the recipient.

The details below describe the intended dial design. Final artwork and photographs will document the result.

A clear hierarchy
A full 1–12 Arabic numeral layout, subdued 13–24 inner numerals, a restrained minute track and subtle concentric separation rings.
Two measured accents
A green bullish candlestick at 12 and a red bearish candlestick at 6. Color is confined to specific symbolic details.
Personal references
The exact Bitfinex leaf motif and “CCO” form part of the design brief. The final vector artwork will be added to the dial record.
Dial inscriptions
“AUTOMATIC” and “200 M” in red. The latter is an intended dial inscription; it is not a verified water-resistance claim for the assembled watch.

05 / Components

The mechanical foundation.

Selected components for a custom design. The record below reflects the current project brief; material confirmation and final testing remain to be documented.

01

TMI NH38 Automatic

TMI (Seiko Group) · No date

24 jewels. 21,600 beats per hour / 3 Hz. Approximately 41 hours of rated power reserve, with automatic and manual winding and hacking seconds. A custom black rotor is specified.

02

36 mm titanium case

Dark gunmetal / brushed finish

Sapphire crystal and an exhibition caseback. Grade 2 titanium is specified in the project brief; material grade and the supplier’s case rating remain to be verified.

03

Dial, hands & strap

Legibility, texture and contrast

A custom matte black field-watch dial with transparent / skeletonized “Phantom” style hands. A black rubber / FKM-style strap, 20 mm wide; exact strap compound to be confirmed.

Water resistance is not yet verified. The nominal 200 m case rating requires supplier confirmation, and the final assembled watch requires pressure testing before a water-resistance specification can be recorded.
